# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Life OS to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

Life OS is a project about wellbeing. Bring the same care to how you treat people
here that the product tries to bring to its users.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient, and respectful.
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the people the product serves.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer via a private
message on the repository (e.g. a GitHub private security advisory or a direct
message). Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially and fairly.
