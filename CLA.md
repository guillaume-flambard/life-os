## Developer Certificate of Origin

By contributing you certify the [Developer Certificate of Origin
1.1](https://developercertificate.org/): that you wrote the contribution or have
the right to submit it, and that it is provided under the project's license.

Sign each commit with `git commit -s`, which appends:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

## Optional relicensing grant

To keep the door open for a future dual-licensed, source-available **sync/backup
server** module (the intended way the project sustains itself, along the lines of
Obsidian/Ente), contributors may additionally grant the maintainer the right to
relicense **their contribution** under such terms.

This grant is **optional** and does not affect the AGPL-3.0 license of the project
as a whole. If you do not include it, your contribution is accepted under AGPL-3.0
only, via the DCO above. To include it, add this line to your PR description:

```
I grant the Life OS maintainer the right to relicense my contribution for the
sync/backup server module under a source-available license.
```

The core application always remains AGPL-3.0. Your data and insights are never part
of any licensing arrangement.
